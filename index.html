shaohan142@gmail.com

About Me

I am a master’s student in Mechanical Engineering at Shanghai University of Engineering Science, advised by Prof. Chen Saixuan. I am a founding member of the Xbotics Embodied Intelligence Community and lead its dexterous-hand direction. My research focuses on in-hand manipulation, spanning tendon-driven mechanism design and multi-loop FOC actuation through reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer.

I prefer to carry a full stack myself: mechanism modelling, embedded actuation, simulation, policy training, and reproduction on hardware.

Research Interests

  • Dexterous hands and in-hand manipulation
  • Tendon-driven and antagonistic mechanisms
  • Reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and VLA
  • Teleoperation and motion retargeting
  • Sim-to-real transfer

Selected Work

  • Antagonistic tendon-driven hand (project lead): 19-DoF five-finger structure, dual-tendon antagonistic drive, STM32 three-loop FOC, and fingertip pressure sensing. National Third Prize, Shanghai First Prize, and the Altium Special Award at the China Graduate Electronics Design Contest.
  • In-hand rotation across hand models: reinforcement learning for O30, O20, and O10 hands in MuJoCo / MJX, including hardware transfer.
  • Xbotics OmniHand learning repository: a nine-chapter open tutorial covering hardware safety, SDK, joint and tactile control, teleoperation, simulation, and learning.
  • Xbotics O6 / O20 consoles: desktop control tools with gesture mapping, action libraries, macro execution, safety limits, and URDF visualization.
  • OmniHand Quest 3 teleoperation: hand-tracking data link, calibration, and 16-to-10 DoF retargeting.
  • UR5 + OmniHand arm-hand coordination: screw insertion and nut pre-engagement.
